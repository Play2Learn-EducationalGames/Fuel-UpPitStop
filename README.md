# Fuel-Up Pit Stop!
The Fuel-Up Pit Stop! game is an interactive educational game for kids of all ages to help them practice identifying GO foods versus SLOW foods. "GO" foods have lots of healthy nutrients that give our bodies and our minds FUEL for the day ahead. "SLOW" foods do not have enough healthy nutrients to fuel us — and can sometimes even make our bodies or minds feel sluggish and slow.

In the game, kids become members of a racing team pit crew where they FUEL the racecar as fast as they can by clicking on the "GO" foods and avoiding the "SLOW" foods to better understand and conceptualize how certain foods fuel our bodies.

The game was developed by the iGrow Study at UNCG -- a team of researchers in the fields of nutrition, kinesiology, and child development & family studies -- as an engaging, evidence-informed tool to spark conversation about food choices at home. The Fuel-Up Pit Stop! game is not intended as a dietary recommendation or clinical intervention — it is an educational activity designed to build awareness and familiarity with nutrient-dense foods in a fun, low-pressure setting.

Ready... Set... GO (not SLOW)!
